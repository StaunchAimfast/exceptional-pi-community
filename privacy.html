EXCEPTIONAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

EXCEPTIONAL is an independent community platform created for Pi Network Pioneers to connect, learn, share knowledge and collaborate.

1. Information We Collect

EXCEPTIONAL may collect information users voluntarily provide, such as usernames, profile details, messages and content submitted through community features. Technical information such as device type, browser information and basic usage data may also be collected where necessary to operate and improve the platform.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate and maintain the platform.
  • Support community participation and communication.
  • Improve user experience and platform security.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide support.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud and unauthorised activity.

3. Pi Network

EXCEPTIONAL may integrate with Pi Network services and the Pi SDK. Pi-related authentication or transactions will be subject to applicable Pi Network terms and policies. EXCEPTIONAL is an independent community project and is not an official Pi Network product or service.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the platform, or where required by applicable law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

EXCEPTIONAL is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

8. Your Choices

You may contact the platform team to request access, correction or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as EXCEPTIONAL develops. Changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the contact details published on our website.
